About the Author

Born in 1984 in Siberia, USSR, Svetlana Bardakova is a contemporary artist and sculptor whose creative journey spans continents, cultures, and artistic disciplines.

Having lived and worked in Russia, Indonesia, Argentina, Bolivia, Chile, Taiwan, England, and Spain, her practice has been shaped by a rich and diverse range of cultural experiences, broadening her artistic perspective and language.

She has organised and participated in international art and sculpture exhibitions in Moscow, Ubud, Buenos Aires, Málaga, and Marbella. Her works are represented in leading galleries and private collections worldwide, including Dubai, Paris, London, Moscow, Saint Paul de Vence, Madrid, Marbella, and Miami.

Working across mixed media, Svetlana explores a wide spectrum of materials — from paper and crayons to plaster, metal, and glass. Constantly seeking new materials and forms of expression, she approaches her practice with an experimental mindset, embracing the freedom to move beyond conventional boundaries between materials, disciplines, and artistic forms.

Through casting and sculptural construction, her practice also extends into wearable art, exploring the dialogue between sculpture, the human body, and fashion. Her vision reaches beyond traditional gallery spaces, towards sculptural creations that can inhabit new contexts — from art installations and exhibitions to couture, red-carpet appearances, and major cultural events.

For Gilded Anatomy, art is a continuous process of translating an inner vision into tangible form, allowing creativity to flow freely between materials, spaces, and life experiences.
